Overview

This knowledge object converts four supplied facts about one candidate diabetic foot ulcer into one guideline-branch result for adjunctive hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBO2). It implements Figure 6 of the 2015 UHMS guideline; it does not diagnose a DFU, calculate Wagner grade, prescribe an HBO2 protocol, or determine coverage.
